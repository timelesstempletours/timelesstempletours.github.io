Felucca on the Nile

Above Luxor at Daybreak

Western Desert Expeditions

Journey into Egypt's Western Desert by 4x4, with overnight camping and unforgettable visits to Deir el-Munira's Roman fortress, Djara Cave, and the White Desert.
